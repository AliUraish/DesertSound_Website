## Body

A voice-controlled smart home runs lights, climate, security, and the theatre from speech. Echo/Alexa, Google Home, and HomePod/Siri are the usual hubs.

"Turn off the lights" or "play the film" only works if the devices are integrated, not just plugged in. We connect the assistants to the real loads, including [home theatre system installation](/service/home-theatre-design-and-installation/), so the command hits the room, not a demo light.
