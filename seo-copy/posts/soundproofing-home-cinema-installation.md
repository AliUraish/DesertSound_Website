## Body

**Keep publish date: Jul 14, 2026**

A home cinema that's loud enough to feel right is also loud enough to leak into the next room, or the street. Soundproofing is a different job from acoustic treatment: treatment shapes how a room sounds inside, soundproofing controls what escapes it.

The basics that actually work: mass (denser walls or added layers), decoupling (breaking the direct path between structures), and sealing gaps at doors and vents where sound leaks fastest. Foam panels on their own don't soundproof a room — that's a common and expensive mistake.

## FAQ

**Is soundproofing the same as acoustic treatment?**
No. Treatment shapes sound inside the room; soundproofing stops it leaking out.

**Do acoustic panels soundproof a room?**
Not by themselves. Panels absorb reflections inside; blocking sound transmission needs mass and decoupling.

**Can soundproofing be added after installation?**
Yes, but it's cheaper and more effective to plan it before construction or wiring is finished.
