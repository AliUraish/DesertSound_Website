## FAQ

**Is a gaming setup different from a home theatre?**
The core build is similar; the tuning differs. We prioritize input lag and audio cues over cinematic surround imaging.

**Does Wi-Fi matter for a gaming room?**
Yes, more than for movie playback. Online multiplayer needs a stable, low-latency connection.

**Can one room do both gaming and movies well?**
Yes, with a control system that switches profiles for source and calibration.
