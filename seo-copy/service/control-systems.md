## FAQ

**What is a control system?**
The hub and interface — panel, app, remote, or voice — that ties lighting, climate, entertainment, and security into one set of commands.

**Can I control everything from my phone?**
Yes, once devices are integrated into the control system, not just connected individually.

**Does voice control need extra hardware?**
Usually just a smart speaker or hub tied into the existing control system, not a full rebuild.

**Can I add rooms or devices later?**
Yes, systems are designed to expand through customization and integration.
