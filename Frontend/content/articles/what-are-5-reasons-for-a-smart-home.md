[Smart homes](/service/smart-home-automation/) earn their keep in five places. The rest is catalogue filler.

### Convenience

Lights, climate, and scenes from a phone, keypad, or voice. A thermostat that follows the day. Lighting that matches how you use the room. That's time you don't spend walking to switches.

### Security

Cameras, sensors, and locks you can check when you're out. Alerts on the phone. Remote lock when you left without checking the door.

### Energy

Heat and cool when the room is used. Lights off in empty rooms. Smart plugs for loads you forget. In Karachi, an AC that isn't running in an empty lounge is the saving that adds up.

### Entertainment

One scene for film: lights, shades, sound. Voice or app instead of three remotes. The theatre becomes part of the house, not a second system.

### Comfort

Air quality devices and routines help some households. Start with lighting, security, and audio — those are the installs that get used. Add the rest later.

Pick a platform and stick to it. A drawer of unused gadgets is not a smart home.
