A home AV system is a display, sound, sources, and the cabling that ties them together. The TV or projector is only one piece.

### Display

TV or projector, sized to the room and seating distance. Resolution and HDR matter. So does whether the room can go dark. A huge screen in a bright lounge is a washed-out screen.

### Audio

Speakers plus a sub. 5.1 is the usual cinema start — fronts, center, surrounds, sub. 7.1 adds side surrounds in a longer room. The center channel carries dialogue. Skip it and voices sit in the picture instead of in front of you.

### Sources

Streaming devices, consoles, a disc player if you still use discs. A receiver or processor switches them so you're not crawling behind the rack.

### Cables and power

HDMI, speaker cable, and power need a planned route. Hidden runs keep the room usable. A surge protector or UPS protects the rack — Karachi power is not gentle. DIY installs skip this and replace gear later.

Spec the system as a whole. A strong display with weak speakers, or the reverse, is how rooms disappoint.
