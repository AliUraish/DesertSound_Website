[Home automation](/service/smart-home-automation/) is remote or scheduled control of lights, climate, security, and appliances — usually from one app or keypad.

### Lighting

On, off, dim, colour. Occupancy and schedules. The most-used install we do, because everyone already uses lights.

### Climate

A thermostat that follows presence and time of day. Useful in Karachi if you don't want the AC on in empty rooms.

### Security

Locks, cameras, sensors. Check the door from work. Alerts when something opens that shouldn't.

### Plugs and appliances

A smart plug makes a lamp or fan schedulable. Kitchen appliances with apps are optional. Schedule heavy loads if your tariff or generator setup cares.

### Voice

Alexa, Google, or Siri for simple commands — if the devices are actually integrated. Voice on a lone speaker is a demo. Voice on lights, climate, and the theatre is the install.

### Irrigation and entertainment

Garden valves on a schedule, weather-aware if the controller supports it. Movie scene: lights down, shades closed, playback started.
