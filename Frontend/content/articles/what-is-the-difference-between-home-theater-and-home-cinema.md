People use the words as synonyms. In practice, a home theatre is a capable system in a living room. A home cinema is a dedicated, treated room.

### Home theatre

Large TV or projector, surround sound, seating you can sit in for two hours, lights you can dim. Often a shared space — lounge by day, films at night.

### Home cinema

A room that exists for playback. Darker finishes, acoustic treatment, usually a projector and a larger screen, seats laid out for one purpose. Soundproofing matters more here because the volume stays up.

### The real differences

- **Purpose.** Theatre: comfort in a room you already use. Cinema: the movie-theatre feel in a room you can close the door on.
- **Build.** Cinema usually means more treatment, more control of light, and a higher install commitment.
- **Space.** Cinema wants a dedicated room. Theatre can live in a multipurpose lounge.

### Which to build

Shared lounge and a real budget: theatre. Spare room and you want the cinema feel: cinema. Both need the same fundamentals — placement, wiring, calibration. The label doesn't fix a bad layout.
