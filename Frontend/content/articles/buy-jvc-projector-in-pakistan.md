Desert Sound supplies and installs JVC projectors in Pakistan. JVC's cinema line is known for contrast and dark-room performance, which is why it shows up in dedicated theatres rather than bright shop floors.

A projector is not a TV you aim at a wall. Throw distance, screen size, ambient light, and the HDMI path decide whether the image holds up.

### Decide these before you buy

- **Room light.** A dark room lets a projector do more. A bright lounge may need a different display, or heavier shade control.
- **Throw and screen.** The projector has to fit the distance you have. Cabinet-on-a-shelf installs usually don't.
- **Source and cabling.** 4K HDR needs a clean HDMI run. Cheap or over-long cable is a common failure.
- **Mount and alignment.** Lens shift and a proper mount beat keystone from a crooked shelf.
