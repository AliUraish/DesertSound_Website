[Audio distribution](/service/audio-distribution/) sends audio from one source — or several — to speakers in different rooms. Same playlist through the house, or a film in the lounge and music in the kitchen.

### How it works

Source (streamer, TV, phone) to an amplifier or matrix, then to speakers per zone. Volume and input are controlled from a wall keypad, remote, or phone. One system replaces a stack of portable speakers in every room.

### Why people install it

- Even coverage instead of one loud room and silence everywhere else
- Per-zone volume so a bedroom isn't stuck at lounge level
- Less clutter: one rack, hidden cable, ceiling or in-wall speakers

### Wired, wireless, or both

- **Wired:** most stable. Best when walls are open or you're building. We hide the runs.
- **Wireless:** faster in a finished home. Needs a solid network — weak Wi-Fi means dropouts.
- **Hybrid:** wire the rooms you can; go wireless where you can't chase cable.

### Homes, shops, offices

Houses use zones for living, dining, outdoor, and a theatre that stays separate. Restaurants and shops need background music they can turn down at the till. Offices need lobbies and meeting rooms that stay intelligible.

Desert Sound plans the zones to the building — Karachi villas, apartments, and commercial floors — then installs and tunes them.
